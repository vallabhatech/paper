\subsection{Training Objective}
For binary classification, a common objective is binary cross-entropy:
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L}=-\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\left[y_i\log(p_i)+(1-y_i)\log(1-p_i)\right],
    \label{eq:bce}
\end{equation}
