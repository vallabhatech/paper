\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}
The evaluation protocol reports accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC, and a confusion matrix, with manipulated video defined as the positive class. For a binary classifier, precision and recall are
\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{Precision}=\frac{TP}{TP+FP}, \qquad
    \mathrm{Recall}=\frac{TP}{TP+FN},
\end{equation}
where $TP$, $FP$, and $FN$ denote true positives, false positives, and false negatives, respectively. Metrics should be computed at the video level using the aggregated video prediction, and this unit should be kept consistent across comparisons. ROC-AUC should use continuous prediction scores rather than thresholded labels.

Cross-dataset generalization is assessed by training on FaceForensics++ and testing on Celeb-DF without fitting to the target test set.
